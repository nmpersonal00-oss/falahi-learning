REALISTIC FLUENCY GOAL
- The built-in plan suggests 90 minutes per day for 150 days (approximately 225 total study hours).
- Daily activities include 15-minute recall, 20-minute grammar, 20-minute native listening,
  20-minute spoken practice, and 15-minute real conversation or role-play.
- The app cannot supply a live native instructor or automatically grade dialect pronunciation.
- Super-fluent/native-level speech by five months is not guaranteed. The milestone goals are aspirational.

DIALECT AND PRIVACY
- Family/Chifak words and your original diacritic-rich flashcards retain their source-specific forms.
- Palestinian and broader Levantine general material is labeled separately.
- Chifak expression drafts are UNCONFIRMED (not certified Deir Dibwan speech).
- App data, progress, profile, and daily checklist save only in this browser. No cloud.
- Chifak original recordings and card images are not included. UI chimes are procedural.
- Web Audio only plays after user interaction, may be silent in some browsers until audio is enabled.
- The Al-Aqsa background references a real licensed Wikimedia Commons photograph while online.
